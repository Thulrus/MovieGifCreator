How this works

The short version

  1. Choose a video. Drop in any video file — a phone clip or a whole movie.
  2. Pick the moment. Play it, then press Set start and Set end around your quote.
  3. Caption it. Auto-caption listens to the clip and types out what’s said. Fix any mistakes, pick a style, and press Make GIF.

You get a GIF and an MP4. Most messaging apps play the MP4 too — it’s smaller and sharper, so send that when you can.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens inside this browser tab, on your computer. Your video, captions and GIFs are never sent to a server.

No. You’re running the app on your own computer, and your videos stay in its sessions/ folder.

Why does the first GIF take longer?

The first time you use it, your browser downloads the tools it needs: the video engine (about 30 MB) and a speech model for auto-captions (41–250 MB, depending on which one you pick in Options). They’re saved by your browser, so next time it starts right away.

Everything runs on your own computer, so a fast computer makes a real difference. A desktop or laptop with Chrome, Edge or Firefox works best; phones can struggle with long videos.

Where are my videos and GIFs kept?

Your original video isn’t copied anywhere — the app reads it straight from where it is on your device. Your clips, captions and GIFs are saved in this browser’s storage (the “Your videos” list in step 1), so you can come back to them later. If you come back to pick a different part of a video, you’ll be asked to choose the file again.

Nothing is synced anywhere else, and clearing your browser’s site data deletes the list, so download the GIFs you want to keep.

Using a shared or public computer?

Tick Don’t save anything under the drop zone in step 1 before you start. The page then keeps nothing personal after you close the tab — no videos, captions, GIFs or settings — so download your GIFs before you leave. You can also clear everything the page has saved from What’s saved on this device at the bottom of the page.

The video engine and speech model it downloads are the same for everyone and contain nothing about you, so they can stay (and save the next person a download) — but you can remove those there too.

Can I use a YouTube link?

Yes — paste it into the box in step 1 and press Get video. Videos you’ve downloaded stay in your list, so you never have to download them twice.

Not on this website. Browsers aren’t allowed to download from YouTube, so this site can only work with video files you already have.

If you’re comfortable with a terminal, you can run the app on your own computer. That version can download straight from YouTube (using yt-dlp):

git clone https://github.com/Thulrus/MovieGifCreator.git
cd MovieGifCreator
python3 -m venv venv
venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt
venv/bin/python app.py

Then open http://127.0.0.1:5050. You’ll also need ffmpeg installed. The full instructions are in the README.

Tips

  • Keep clips short — 3 to 10 seconds makes a GIF that loads fast and fits in a text message.
  • If a GIF comes out too big, pick a smaller Size or Smoothness.
  • Your work saves automatically. Come back to a video from the list any time and your range, captions and style will still be there.
